3. Put it on your Switch

    
    
    
      
  1. Copy mkw-launcher.nro to /switch/ on your SD card.
    2. 
      
  2. Copy your disc image to /iso/ on the SD card (ISO or WBFS; the launcher can't read RVZ, so convert it to ISO in Dolphin first).
    3. 
      
  3. Download the Retro Rewind  full pack and copy its RetroRewind6 folder to /switch/kartpad/retro-rewind/.
    4. 
      
  4. On the Switch, hold R while starting any game, open MKW Launcher and follow its setup. It imports your disc from /iso/.
    5. 
    

    
When Retro Rewind updates, build here again and replace the two files. Mods go in /switch/kartpad/mods/ and are switched on in the launcher's Mods tab, no rebuild needed.
